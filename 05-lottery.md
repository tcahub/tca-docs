# 05 · Lottery eligibility, as a direction

The lottery is not open. It is a holder perk: eligibility comes from holding, not from buying tickets.

1. Hold at least **10** Renaiss SBTs. An SBT is a non-transferable token, checked on the theme’s own page. How the count is taken follows the formal release.
2. Also hold the matching theme token. Both conditions must hold together. The amount to hold is still open.

The intent is a reveal about every **24 hours**. The prize pool can track trading volume. The amount is not fixed in this draft.

For now the page points to X only — [@tapecards](https://x.com/tapecards). It does not connect a wallet, check holdings, or take entries.

Draft: after the Renaiss integration is settled, login may switch to or also support Renaiss accounts. Their site mostly uses their own accounts. The exact change waits on that result.
