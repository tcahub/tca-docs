# 06 · Council · Decision rights

Every theme token gets its own community and decision team. At the protocol layer, a 12-seat Council is forming. It decides which theme launches next, how treasury funds are spent, and how fees and rules evolve.

A Council exists to decide whether something should happen. Seats are screened from the community and added only after an internal human review.

| Item | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **Seats** | Tentatively **12**, screened from the community with human review. Hitting a public bar does not by itself put someone on the list. |
| **Votes on** | Buybacks, burns, and which theme or series to support, including what launches next and how treasury funds are used. |
| **No vote on** | Day-to-day card buying to restock, which follows the set strategy. |
| **Proposals** | Trading fees fund the treasury. Buyback-and-burn proposals are raised only while fees keep coming in. If a proposal fails or is not carried out, that round does not happen. |
| **Themes** | Each theme token can also have its own community council for direction and community matters, voted by its pledgers and holders. |

The first 12 seats are screened from the community. The public follow link only opens [@tapecards](https://x.com/tapecards). It does not connect a wallet.

Seat thresholds, the voting tool, and the pass rules follow the formal release.
