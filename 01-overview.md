# 01 · Position

**One line:** Make TCG more fun.

**What TCA is.** TCA is a launchpad that brings graded physical cards on-chain: each card theme becomes one slot, and each slot issues one token — asset tokenization, issued by theme.

The token is the tool, not the point. It exists so card lovers can sweep up the cards they love and build the community around them. TCA serves the cards, and the people who collect them.

Tokens can burn. **Cards stay forever.**

This stage is paper. This page does not connect a wallet, does not buy or sell, and does not run a lottery.

## Proven, then broken

Cards plus meme energy is a validated formula — several TCG-adjacent tokens have crossed $10M in market cap. None of them kept it. The idea was not wrong. The structure was.

- **The whale exit.** Early players buy at a tiny market cap, then sell into every rally.
- **The copycat sea.** Impostors shred money meant for one chart across ten.
- **Nothing underneath.** A pure meme token is backed by attention, and attention leaves.

TCA keeps a theme, a community, and a token, and rebuilds the rest: a vault of graded cards behind every launch, real people required before a token exists, and rules that slow the dump.

Each of those failures has one countermeasure the market can check: quota release, one contract and one slot plus the opening anti-snipe, and card backing (CBR). See [04-buy-protection.md](./04-buy-protection.md) and [08-supply-backing-liquidity.md](./08-supply-backing-liquidity.md).

## Why now

Four things are true at the same time.

1. **Grading is standardized.** PSA, BGS, and CGC give a card a public, verifiable grade.
2. **Custody is mature.** Networks like Renaiss provide custody and FMV pricing. Ownership and yield finally separate.
3. **Price discovery is ready.** A theme token gives every IP a continuous quote. A card's value is visible between sales, not only at them.
4. **The majors are moving in.** Coinbase has teased its own TCG product and put a card platform's token on its listing roadmap. On Solana, tokenized-card venues have already crossed $1B in cumulative volume.

## Theme slots

One theme, one slot, one token. Each slot is a graded slab: the card of that theme inside, its launch progress on the label. The launchpad frame is a prototype. Slots are not live yet.

Pikachu, Charizard, and Gengar are example slots. Eevee, Rayquaza, Luffy (One Piece), and a Naruto series are queued. More themes are decided by the Council. These names are illustrative only. A name marks a slot. It does not mean a card is in custody or that a token has been issued. No theme is tradable yet.

## Someone is always bidding

A collectible's worst day is not a lower price. It is no price at all. Renaiss is building a Collectible Finance Network for real-world collectibles.

1. **Fees become cards.** Part of every theme's trading fees flows to its treasury, which buys graded cards of that theme on Renaiss. Purchases are public.
2. **Cards become backing.** Bought cards go into the theme vault and lift its Card Backing Ratio.
3. **Owners get an exit.** A listing priced within reason has a standing bid.

Traders pay fees, fees buy cards, and cards back the token. The public word is "partner."
