# 06 · DAO · Decision rights

The DAO is how **decisions are confirmed** — what will be done. Not everyone can join. A voting entry will be reserved. The team turns eligibility on by hand, after review.

| Item | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **Members** | Tentatively **12** seats. Every seat is community-sourced and is added to the whitelist only after an internal human review (hitting a public bar does not by itself put someone on the list). |
| **Put to a vote** | Buybacks, burns, and which theme or series to support. |
| **Not put to a vote** | Day-to-day card buys that restock under the strategy already set. |
| **Funds and proposals** | Trading fees go into the treasury. Buyback and burn proposals are brought back only while fees keep coming in. If a proposal does not pass, that buyback and burn does not run. |
| **Direction** | Once fees are in the treasury, buybacks, burns, and theme support follow passed proposals. The lean is fees toward buying and supporting the floor, rather than burns alone. |

The figures for a seat threshold, the voting tool, and the passing rules are still draft and follow the official release.
